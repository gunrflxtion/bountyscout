The problem

Security researchers waste days auditing programs they cannot even submit to: a $100 pay-to-submit fee discovered after the audit, a 120-point reputation gate, a KYC wall at payout, or a scope outside their stack. Program pages hide these gates in long rules text. BountyScout reads the page for you and surfaces the decision facts first: reward, fee, reputation gate, KYC, scope — scored 0–100 with a GO / CAUTION / SKIP verdict and the reasons behind it.

How the AI works

  1. Extract. A deterministic pattern + keyword pipeline reads the program text: reward amounts ($ and USDC), submit fees (including “credits = US dollars”), reputation gates (“120+ points”), KYC mentions, scope (EVM / Move / Rust / Web), deadlines, and free-submission signals. “Out of scope” clauses are excluded so a program that says “EVM out of scope” is not misread as EVM scope.
  2. Score. A transparent additive model (0–100). Free submission +20, high reward up to +15, EVM scope +10, early/live +5; fees −25/−35, reputation gates −20/−25, KYC −5. Hard caps: an entry fee of $50+ or a 100+ reputation gate caps the score at SKIP — a hunter with zero reputation literally cannot enter.
  3. Explain. Every point adjustment is listed as a human-readable reason, so the verdict is auditable. The result exports as a Markdown brief with a pre-audit checklist.
  4. Optional second opinion. With your own OpenRouter key, a free-tier LLM re-reads the same text and either agrees or disagrees with reasons. The deterministic verdict is always shown first; the LLM never overrides it silently.

Honest limits: the extractor reads what the text says — a gate the page does not mention cannot be detected. Always verify fee, reputation and KYC live on the program page before auditing. BountyScout is a triage tool, not a guarantee.

Why it matters

In one research round, 3 out of 11 real programs turned out to be unauditable for a free-first hunter with zero reputation — despite headline rewards up to $500,000. Each one cost hours of reading and audit work before the gate was found. BountyScout finds the gate in seconds, so the hours go to programs that can actually pay.
